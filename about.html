Our story

Since 2017.

“A community of doers, for a more open tomorrow.”

Inovus Labs is the Innovation & Entrepreneurship Development Centre at KJCMT, run by students with support from the Kerala Startup Mission.

What began as a few curious learners is now a space where students build hardware, ship software and start ventures, nine generations and counting.
