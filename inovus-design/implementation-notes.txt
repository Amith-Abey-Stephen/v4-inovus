IMPLEMENTATION
Share Header, Footer, StampButton, OutlineButton, TextLink, Field, CheckboxChip, StatusBadge, EventCard, ResourceRow, ProfileCard, DocumentRow, ArticleLayout, Dialog and EmptyState.
Use the exact colors and spacing in design-tokens.json rather than sampling texture pixels.
Normalize incidental generated differences in red, heading size, card spacing and footer links.
Desktop: max 1280px content; 12 columns; 24px gutters.
Tablet 768–1023px: 8 columns; 2-column card layouts; 32px margins.
Mobile below 768px: 4 columns; single-column content; 20px margins; full-width primary form actions; accordion navigation.
Use Inter 800 for headings, Inter 400/500 for text and Caveat for short annotations.
Use 16–18px body type, 1.5–1.65 line height, roughly 65-character reading columns.
Keep controls at least 48px tall. Use semantic links and buttons, persistent labels, visible focus, keyboard-operable menus, focus trapping and Escape dismissal for dialogs.
Show error text with an icon, retain form values after failure, prevent duplicate submission and announce success/loading with live regions.
Never offer registration for a completed event. Use View recap; upcoming registration needs confirmed event data.
Use restrained 120–180ms color/opacity transitions. Disable optional movement when reduced motion is requested.
Validate actual typography, contrast, keyboard flow, screen-reader behavior and responsive layouts after implementation. Raster images do not establish accessibility compliance.
