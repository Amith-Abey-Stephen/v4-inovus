Good questions

Before you jump in.

Follow on WhatsApp ↗
Do I need experience?

No. Most of us started with zero. You learn by building alongside others.

Who can join?

Inovus is run by and for KJCMT students. Students from other colleges can join outreach sessions and events.

How much time does it take?

As much as you can give. Sessions, workshops and build nights happen through the year.

What happens after I reach out?

A coordinator will get back to you about upcoming sessions and how to get involved.
